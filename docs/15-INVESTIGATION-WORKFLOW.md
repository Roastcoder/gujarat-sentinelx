## 2. Case Dossier & Chain of Custody
1. **Case Association:** Detections are formally linked to digital police case files (e.g. `INV-2026-0402`).
2. **Photographic Evidence Preservation:** High-resolution crops of license plates and vehicle frames are indexed with cryptographic SHA-256 integrity hashes to safeguard admissible court evidence.
3. **Forensic Field Notes:** Authoring officers attach real-time investigative notes tracking suspect movements across municipal lines.
